# EcoDNA Insights

Build a highly advanced, modern, attractive, responsive web application called:

“EcoDNA AI – Environmental Digital DNA Platform”

==================================================

1. PROJECT CONCEPT

==================================================

EcoDNA AI is an AI-powered Environmental Digital DNA Platform

that creates a unique environmental identity for every monitored

location.

The system continuously analyzes environmental factors such as:

- Air pollution

- Water quality

- Biodiversity

- Waste level

- Weather conditions

- Green cover

- Rainfall

- Other environmental indicators

Based on these environmental factors, the system generates an:

“Environmental DNA Score”

from 0–100.

The AI analyzes current and historical environmental data to:

- Assess environmental health

- Identify pollution risks

- Detect environmental hotspots

- Predict future environmental conditions

- Recommend suitable environmental recovery actions

- Simulate the expected improvement after recovery actions

The system must NOT look like a simple pollution monitoring

dashboard.

The main innovation is:

MONITOR → ANALYZE → IDENTIFY → PREDICT → RECOMMEND → SIMULATE

==================================================

2. CORE INNOVATIONS

==================================================

The application must clearly demonstrate these innovations:

1. Environmental Digital DNA

Every location receives a unique Environmental DNA identity

based on its environmental conditions.

2. Environmental DNA Score

A 0–100 score representing the overall environmental health

of a location.

3. AI Environmental Prediction

AI analyzes historical environmental patterns and predicts

future environmental health.

4. AI Pollution Hotspot Mapping

The system identifies high-risk environmental zones and

visualizes them on an interactive map.

5. AI Environmental Recovery Recommendation

When environmental degradation is detected, the system

recommends suitable recovery actions.

6. Environmental Recovery Simulation

The system simulates how the Environmental DNA Score could

change after applying different recovery actions.

7. Location Environmental Identity

Each location has its own environmental profile containing

its Environmental DNA, risks, trends, predictions and

recovery potential.
